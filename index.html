05 / MODEL GOVERNANCE

Method, assumptions & AI

This is an independently built operational demonstration, not a product integrated into a property company's billing or EV charging system.

Reconciliation contract

Each charging session must have a unique ID, an approved charger-to-site mapping and contiguous cumulative meter intervals covering its complete duration. Every interval must fit wholly inside a single applicable tariff window. Costs are calculated with integer energy values and micro-pound tariff rates, rounded to pennies once per session.

Records with insufficient evidence are held for review. A crossing tariff window cannot be priced without finer-grained readings.

What the totals exclude

VAT, standing charges, charger losses, property supply-meter reconciliation, solar and battery apportionment, customer invoicing and regulatory treatment are outside this demonstration.

Meter readings alone do not establish a property's actual grid procurement costs.
